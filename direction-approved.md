## User selection

User-approved direction, 3 September 2026:

> “A 的专业视觉 + C 的七种 Engineering Mode + B 的小型 Growth Stage 标签。 然后由于不知道是男生还是女生做这个，我想到时候结果就是随机的，有男有女可以吗？”

Follow-up colour decision:

> “每一种 Engineering Mode用的颜色主题色调能不能不要一样，可以是 红橙黄绿青蓝紫，选合适的，但是要区分开。”

## Implementation interpretation

- Preserve the established HKU-inspired green/white system for global navigation, controls, charts, and the formal report.
- Add an editorial character-led identity card at the top of the result page.
- Provide seven non-fixed Current Engineering Modes: Problem Framer, Project Navigator, Team Connector, Practical Builder, Prototype Explorer, Solution Storyteller, and Systems Integrator.
- Add a compact four-step Engineering Growth Stage label: Exploring, Building, Practising, and Integrating.
- Each mode has two randomly selected character presentations. The choice is visual only, is not inferred from the learner, and stays stable for one completed result.
- Give the seven modes distinct red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, and purple accent systems while retaining the common editorial illustration language.
