## Practice evidence review

PE01–02 mechanics: assembly/alignment and fault adjustment.
PE03–04 CAD: editable dimensioned model and mating-part fit.
PE05–06 fabrication: approved setup and test-piece-driven adjustment.
PE07–08 electronics: safe low-voltage measurements and systematic fault finding.
PE09–10 software: input/output requirement and regression-checked bug fix.
PE11–12 embedded: hardware input/output and timing/state diagnosis.
PE13–14 sensors/IoT: reference calibration/repeatability and unreliable-stream recovery.
PE15–16 AI/vision: held-out evaluation and condition-dependent limitations.
PE17–18 integration: interface testing and controlled failure recovery.

Every pair covers doing and checking a distinct task. All four response values are present, unique, ordered consistently, and translated; feedback remains task-specific. These are self-reports, not uploaded or externally verified evidence; they do not alter core 0–100 scores. Four levels are retained to avoid inventing a fifth experience category.

## Shared question and layout review

B01–15 retain the same five frequency anchors, without replacing them with technical independence descriptions. T01–09 retain full independence definitions for accessible names and the expandable five-level explanation. Short anchors appear outside the numeric boxes; selecting a level shows its complete meaning. Long responsibility, judgment and Pro answers use full-width rows rather than narrow alternating columns. Interest/growth selections retain their exclusivity rules and coverage.

Existing constructs remain approximate self-reflection: broad independence prompts bundle skills, judgments can invite socially desirable responses, and no reliability or validity study is claimed. A wording/scoring redesign would need a separate version and learner pilot; this change does not claim psychometric validation.

## Background interaction

Six existing compact astronaut assets are retained, clustered within the top 1080px on desktop and top 1650px on stacked phones. Different slow closed loops avoid synchronized movement. One satellite follows one orbit. Passive pointer movement/taps nudge a nearby astronaut away with damped momentum and a slow return; translation is bounded.
